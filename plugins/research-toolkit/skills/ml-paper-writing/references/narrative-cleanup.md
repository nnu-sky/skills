# Narrative self-check and examples

The default narrative principles live in `../SKILL.md`. Use this short reference when revising report-like prose or resolving recurring writing problems. Limit the check to the requested scope and necessary adjacent context.

## Four questions

1. Does the passage advance a scientific question, mechanism, finding, or implication, or mainly record work performed?
2. Would deleting this detail change the scientific interpretation? If not, does it have a clear reproduction role? Put necessary settings in the protocol, execution records in artifacts, and delete redundant text.
3. Has this explanation or limitation already appeared? Can an accurately scoped claim replace an additional negative qualification?
4. Do the prose, display, and caption repeat the same numbers or explanation? Give each a distinct role.

## Examples

| Report-like or defensive prose | Scientific prose |
| --- | --- |
| We loaded a checkpoint, copied its parameters, ran the two branches, and recorded their differences at each step. | Changing only the backward rule at a shared forward state produces different gradients and separates the optimization trajectories. |
| This does not show that the phenomenon occurs everywhere or establish its prevalence during training. | The construction establishes local existence of the phenomenon. |
| This is an analytic illustration, not a field measured from a trained model. | The analytic illustration shows how mismatch changes local update-field geometry. |
| The measured grid is finite and cannot establish a guarantee over the entire domain. | The sampled errors remain below the uniform theoretical bound. |

Adapt examples to the actual evidence; they are not stock sentences to insert. Keep controls needed for causal interpretation and assumptions needed for a theorem. Do not remove mixed or negative findings, imply statistical equivalence from similar averages, or ban particular words or sentence forms. No fixed quota of numbers, paragraph length, or limitation sentences is required.
